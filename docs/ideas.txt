  - Milestone alerts: 50/100 for batters, 5-wicket hauls for bowlers

Draft Improvements
  - Format filter in draft: Test draft shows Test specialists first (endurance/technique)
  - Stats column: show batting avg / bowling avg in addition to OVR for ODI/Test drafts

Phase 6 — Monetisation
  - StoreKit (iOS) + Google Play Billing
  - "Pro" subscription tier — feature gate: advanced analytics, full history, rename packs
  - Auction mode (alternative to snake draft — bid-based squad building)
  - One-time content pack purchases

Phase 7 — Engagement
  - Commentary / flavor-text feed during matches
  - Achievements / badges (first century, 5-wicket haul, unbeaten season, etc.)
  - Daily / weekly challenges
  - Advanced analytics screens (Pro)

Phase 8D — Auction Mode
  - Bid-based squad building as an alternative to snake/mega draft
  - Format-agnostic; can ship independently of format work

Phase 9 — Social / Multiplayer  (Pro flagship)
  - Multiplayer leagues (friends, shared simulation)
  - Global leaderboards / ranked seasons
  - Trade system between human-managed franchises

Phase 10 — v1.0 Stable
  - Full QA across devices (iPhone 15/16/17, iPad, various Android)
  - Localization (EN first; consider Hindi/Urdu for India market)
  - App Store optimization
  - Launch marketing
