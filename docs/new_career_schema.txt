New Career — Options & Data Model
===================================

CAREER CREATION WIZARD (new-career.tsx)
----------------------------------------

Step 1: Competition
  ┌─────────────────────────┐  ┌─────────────────────────┐
  │   Indian T20 League     │  │     International       │
  │  franchise T20 league   │  │  T20 / ODI / Test       │
  └─────────────────────────┘  └─────────────────────────┘

Step 2 (International only): Options page — all three segments on one screen
  Match Format:   T20 · ODI · Test
  Career Mode:    World Tournament · Bilateral Series
  Squad:          Current Roster · All-Time Greats

  Notes:
  - World Tournament defaults selected when entering this step
  - Current Roster = real 2026 squads, no draft, straight to season
  - All-Time Greats = mega draft from 350+ legends
  - Tournament + All-Time → world city franchises (Mumbai Monsoons, etc.)
  - Bilateral + All-Time → national team drafts (you draft as/onto a nation)

Step 3 (Bilateral only): Pick opponent + series length (1, 3, or 5)
  Note: all N matches always play out regardless of who clinches first

Step 4: Team/Franchise picker
  IPL:               10 Indian city franchises (Chennai Cholas, Mumbai Mavericks, etc.)
  Intl current:      10 national teams (India, Australia, England, ...)
  Intl alltime tour: 10 world city franchises (Mumbai Monsoons, Sydney Sharks, ...)
  Intl alltime bilat: 10 national teams (you pick one; opponent picked separately)

Step 5: Difficulty  (Easy / Medium / Hard)
Step 6: Career Name (optional, auto-generated if blank)
  → "Start Tournament" / "Start Series" / "Create New League"


API PAYLOAD (POST /api/careers/)
---------------------------------

Field             Type              Values / Notes
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
competition       string            "ipl" | "international"
match_format      string            "t20" | "odi" | "test"
career_mode       string            "league" | "tournament" | "bilateral"
user_team_name    string            see Team Lists below
difficulty        string            "easy" | "medium" | "hard"
draft_pool_type   string            see Draft Pools below
series_length     int | null        1 | 3 | 5 (bilateral only, else null)
opponent_name     string | null     national team name (bilateral only, else null)
name              string            career display name


DRAFT POOL TYPES
-----------------

draft_pool_type       Used for                          Team list
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
rosters2026           IPL (always)                      10 IPL franchises
international_current Intl current roster (any mode)    10 national teams
alltime_t20_intl      Intl all-time T20 tournament       10 world franchises
alltime_odi           Intl all-time ODI tournament       10 world franchises
alltime_test          Intl all-time Test tournament      10 world franchises

Note: bilateral all-time drafts use the same pool types (alltime_odi etc.)
but `user_team_name` is a national team name and `opponent_name` is required.
There is no separate bilateral-only pool — bilateral and tournament share pools.

VALID CAREER MODE COMBINATIONS
--------------------------------

competition   career_mode   draft_pool_type        user_team_name from
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ipl           league        rosters2026            IPL Franchises
international tournament    international_current  National Teams
international tournament    alltime_t20_intl       World Franchises
international tournament    alltime_odi            World Franchises
international tournament    alltime_test           World Franchises
international bilateral     international_current  National Teams
international bilateral     alltime_t20_intl       National Teams *
international bilateral     alltime_odi            National Teams *
international bilateral     alltime_test           National Teams *

* Bilateral all-time: you draft as/onto a national team (not a world franchise),
  because bilateral is always two specific nations facing each other.


TOURNAMENT PLAYOFF STRUCTURE
------------------------------

T20 / ODI tournament:
  Round-robin (9 rounds, every team plays every other team once)
  → Semifinal 1: 1st vs 4th
  → Semifinal 2: 2nd vs 3rd
  → Final

Test tournament:
  Round-robin (9 rounds)
  → Final: 1st vs 2nd (no semis)

IPL league:
  14 rounds (standard IPL double round-robin with scheduling padding)
  → Qualifier 1 (Q1): 1st vs 2nd
  → Eliminator: 3rd vs 4th
  → Qualifier 2 (Q2): Q1 loser vs Eliminator winner
  → Final: Q1 winner vs Q2 winner


BILATERAL SERIES COMPLETION
-----------------------------

All matches always play out regardless of who clinches first:
  Best of 1: 1 match total
  Best of 3: 3 matches total
  Best of 5: 5 matches total
Winner = team with most wins after all matches; tie = draw.


ENGINE CONSTANTS (constants.py / theme.ts)

Python: INTERNATIONAL_TEAMS_LIST, INTERNATIONAL_TEAM_META
        WORLD_TEAMS_LIST, WORLD_TEAM_META
        TEAM_META (IPL)

TypeScript: INTERNATIONAL_TEAM_NAMES, InternationalTeamColors
            WORLD_TEAM_NAMES, WorldTeamColors
            TEAM_NAMES, TeamColors (IPL)

teamMetaByName(name) checks all three tables in order: IPL → International → World.
