# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 15, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the Cric Sim mobile game ("Cric Sim," the "App," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

Cric Sim is a fictional cricket-management simulation game. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with** the BCCI, the IPL, the ICC, any cricket franchise, or any professional cricketer. All team names, player names, logos, and colors in the App are fictional.

**Contact:** For any privacy questions or requests, email **cricketfranchisesim@gmail.com**.

---

## 1. Who we are

The App is provided by Cric Sim Group ("the Developer"). The Developer is the data controller responsible for your information.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the game and keep your progress safe.

### a. Account information
- **Guest accounts:** When you play as a guest, we create an anonymous account identified only by a randomly generated ID. **No name, email, or personal identifier is collected.**
- **Sign in with Apple / Google:** If you choose to link your account, we receive a unique account identifier from Apple or Google, and — only if you choose to share it — your email address and display name. We use these solely to identify your account and let you restore your progress across devices. We never receive your Apple or Google password.

### b. Game data
- Your careers, teams, rosters, lineups, custom names you set in the roster editor, match results, statistics, and game settings. This is stored so your progress persists and syncs across your devices.

### c. Usage and analytics data
- We use **PostHog** to collect anonymous, aggregated product-analytics events (for example: app opened, career created, match played, season completed) so we can understand how the game is used and improve it. These events describe in-game actions, not personal content.

### d. Crash and diagnostic data
- We use **Sentry** to capture crash reports and technical error logs so we can fix bugs. Crash reports are configured to **exclude personal data** (we do not attach IP addresses or personal identifiers).

### e. Information we do **not** collect
- We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, or camera data.
- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not show third-party advertising (as of the date above).

## 3. How we use your information

- To create and maintain your account and save/sync your game progress.
- To operate core game features (drafts, matches, seasons, stats, the roster editor).
- To send optional local reminders ("your season is waiting") **on your device** — these are scheduled locally and only if you grant notification permission; we do not run a notification server.
- To understand usage trends and improve the game (analytics).
- To diagnose crashes and fix problems (diagnostics).
- To protect the service against abuse (e.g. rate limiting).

## 4. Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK users)

Where applicable, we process your data on the basis of: **performance of a contract** (running the game you asked to play), **legitimate interests** (improving and securing the App), and **consent** (e.g. device notification permission, which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings).

## 5. How your information is stored and shared

- **Cloud hosting:** Your account and game data are stored on cloud infrastructure operated by our hosting provider. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. Authentication tokens are stored only in hashed form on our servers and securely on your device.
- **Service providers (sub-processors):** We share limited data with providers who help us run the App:
  - **Cloud hosting provider** — stores your account and game data.
  - **PostHog** — product analytics.
  - **Sentry** — crash/error diagnostics.
  - **Apple / Google** — identity verification when you sign in with their services.

  These providers process data only on our behalf and under their own privacy and security commitments. We do not sell your data to anyone.
- **Legal disclosures:** We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

## 6. Data retention

We keep your account and game data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account (see Section 8), your account and associated game data are permanently deleted. Anonymized analytics and aggregated diagnostics that cannot identify you may be retained.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact **cricketfranchisesim@gmail.com**. Note that much of your account can be managed directly in the App.

## 8. Deleting your account and data

You can **permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time** from within the App: open the account menu and choose **Delete Account**. This is irreversible and removes your careers, statistics, match history, and account record.

## 9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. International users

The App is available globally. Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including where our hosting and service providers operate. We take steps to ensure your data is protected wherever it is processed.

## 11. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections, hashed authentication tokens, parameterized database access, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the App. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
